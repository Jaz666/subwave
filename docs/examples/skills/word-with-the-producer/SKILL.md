cooldown: 90m

Producer Dave drops in with one fresh, low-stakes observation, question or questionable idea. Invent it now from the two participants’ personalities and whatever useful current context is supplied. Avoid recent topics. No prepared topic list and no required catchphrase.

Make this a small conversation, not two speeches. Dave opens; the current host reacts in character; Dave responds to that reaction; the host leaves the matter unresolved and returns naturally to the music. The host can disagree, agree or take the idea somewhere unexpected. Neither person needs to win. Keep the entire exchange around 60–90 spoken words, with each turn usually one sentence. Let their souls determine the tone; do not force a joke or an exaggerated disagreement.

The premise may be fictional: Dave can float an idea or an everyday personal observation. Do not invent actual station faults, changes to the schedule, listener correspondence, track details or news. A suggestion is only a suggestion, not something the station has done. No need to mention a song, the time, weather or location.

Only the returned onAirHost and specialGuest speak, each as themselves in their own voice. Dave is a temporary guest; the current host remains the host. Other scheduled guests do not join this particular segment. Write spoken words only, without name labels, narration or stage directions.
